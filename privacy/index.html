CaddieBot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 App: CaddieBot (com.caddiebot.app) on Google Play Developer: Lionel Ng Contact: support@caddiebot.app

The short version

CaddieBot is a golf GPS and caddie app. It uses your phone's location to show where you are on the hole and how far it is to the green and hazards. Your location stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't sell or share your data.

What the app uses, and why

Location (precise GPS). This shows which hole you're on, your distances to the green and hazards, and moves you to the next hole. It's used only while the app is open or a round is running; a notification shows while a round is active. CaddieBot never asks for background ("all the time") location, and your position is never sent to us or anyone else.

Motion sensors. These detect swings and putts so strokes can be counted. They're processed only on your phone.

Voice. Advice is spoken with your phone's text-to-speech. CaddieBot doesn't use the microphone or camera.

What stays on your phone

These are kept in the app's private storage: your settings, club distances, swing setup, rounds and scorecards, a per-round log of positions for reviewing a round, and the course maps you've downloaded. Cloud backup is turned off, so none of it is copied off your phone.

How long it's kept: until you delete it. To erase everything, uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings > Apps > CaddieBot > Storage > Clear storage.

What leaves your phone

The app connects to the internet only for these things:

  • Weather: the US National Weather Service (api.weather.gov). The app sends the location of the golf course you picked, not your own position, to get the wind for plays-like yardages.
  • Course maps. The app downloads course maps and aerial photos from our course server. For paid courses, it sends your Google Play purchase receipt so the server can confirm the unlock. That receipt doesn't include your name or email, and the server doesn't keep it.
  • Payments: Google Play. The 99-cent unlock is bought through Google Play, and Google handles the payment under Google's Privacy Policy. We never see your card or payment details, only whether the unlock was bought.

Like any website, these services see your phone's internet (IP) address and basic device info, such as the app version, when the app connects. We don't use that to identify you, and our course server doesn't keep logs of it beyond what's needed to run the service.

If you use the Share button to send a scorecard, it goes to whichever app you pick. We don't receive it.

Your choices and rights

  • You can turn location off at any time in Android settings. The app just won't show live distances.
  • You can delete all app data any time, as described above.
  • Because we don't hold any of your personal data on our servers, there's nothing for us to look up, correct or delete. If you have a question or request anyway, email us and we'll answer.

Children

CaddieBot is meant for adult golfers and isn't directed at children under 13.

Safety

Yardages, plays-like distances and club advice are estimates. Always play safely and follow course rules.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date.
